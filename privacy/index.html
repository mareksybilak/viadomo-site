Viadomo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Viadomo is a news reader for macOS. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: Viadomo does not collect, store or share any of your data on our side. Everything the app keeps stays on your Mac.

Who is responsible

Viadomo is developed by Marek Sybilak, Poland (“we”). Contact for privacy questions: marek.sybilak@datalog.pl.

What we collect

Nothing. Viadomo has no user accounts with us, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive your feeds, your reading history, your logins or any other information about you.

What stays on your Mac

To work, the app stores the following only on your device, inside its private app container:

  • the feeds you subscribe to, your groups and your settings;
  • downloaded articles, their images and their read/starred state;
  • for sites you log in to (such as Medium or a newspaper you subscribe to), that site’s session data (cookies and similar website data) in a separate, isolated store for each site.

You can delete this data at any time: remove a feed, or log out of or remove a site in the app’s Accounts panel. Deleting the app from Applications does not remove its data on macOS; to remove everything, also delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/app.viadomo.mac.

Your logins

When you log in to Medium or another site, you do so on that site’s own login page, shown inside the app. Viadomo never sees or stores your password. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that happens between you and Apple or Google. The app keeps only the session that the site gives your browser, so that it can download the articles you are entitled to read. This session is sent only to that site.

Connections the app makes

Viadomo connects directly from your Mac to:

  • the websites and feeds you subscribe to, to fetch new articles, their full text and images;
  • Medium and other sites you choose to log in to.

These sites receive what any web browser would send them (for example your IP address and a browser identifier). Their own privacy policies apply to that. Viadomo does not route any of this through us.

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any data, because we do not have any. When you use the Share or Copy Link buttons, the link goes only where you send it.

Children

Viadomo is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not process your personal data, there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct or delete. The data on your Mac is fully under your control. If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to answer you, and we delete them when the matter is closed. Under the GDPR you may ask us about this and you may lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
